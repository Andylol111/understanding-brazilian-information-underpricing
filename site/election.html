Price differences between Polymarket and Kalshi

For each race listed on both platforms: the cost of buying one candidate on one platform and the other candidate on the other, at the quoted asks and after both platforms' fees. The net edge is $1 minus that cost; above zero, holding both sides pays out more than it costs whoever wins.

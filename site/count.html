Read before you rely on any number
  • No early / Election Day split: the Florida, South Carolina and Ohio statewide and House models do not separate early and mail ballots from Election Day. Early ballots, counted first, distort the share counted and the chance until Election Day precincts report.
  • NC and GA House: the models are far more Democratic than markets at 50–70% counted; cause not yet verified. Read with caution.
  • Markets: some Polymarket prices on safe races come from one-sided order books and read inverted; those are shown as “unavailable (one-sided book)”.
  • Not an official call; not financial advice. Shares are of the two-party vote; “counted” is the model’s estimate of the share of votes in.
